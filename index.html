Heesung Kim

Ph.D. Candidate in Economics
University of Michigan

I am an Economics Ph.D. candidate at the University of Michigan. My research lies at the intersection of development economics and labor economics, with a focus on online labor markets, hiring, and digital job opportunities.

Before graduate school, I was a Research Associate at the Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis. I received my B.S. in Economics and Mathematics from the University of Michigan.
